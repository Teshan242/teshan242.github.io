About Me

I am a passionate undergraduate student specializing in Cyber Security at the Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology (SLIIT), Faculty of Computing. Since 2024, I have been pursuing my degree with a strong focus on information security, network security, and ethical hacking.

My journey in cyber security began with a fascination for understanding how systems can be protected against threats, which has evolved into a deep passion for identifying vulnerabilities and implementing robust security measures. I enjoy working with security tools and am constantly expanding my knowledge in penetration testing, incident response, and security compliance.

My career goal is to become a professional Security Analyst and Cyber Security Consultant, helping organizations protect their digital assets and maintain robust security postures against evolving threats.
